# Lossless, de-warped capture at ≥300 DPI

The source book is captured as lossless PNG/TIFF at ≥300 DPI effective over the text area,
de-warped — not as JPEG phone photos. Farsi nuqta are ~4 px at 300 DPI and JPEG destroys
them below ~3 px, and Google Vision cannot repair that damage. The book is irreplaceable,
so a lossy master is an unrecoverable error. A phone is acceptable only if it meets this
bar (two half-page shots at close range, or a ≥16 MP camera at ≤26 cm); otherwise a
flatbed or scanning service.
